Good to Know

Questions we hear a lot.

What areas does BREN Landscaping serve?

We're based in Fairfield, CT and work throughout the surrounding towns — Southport, Westport, Easton, Weston, Trumbull, Stratford, Monroe, Shelton, Milford, and neighboring Fairfield County communities.

Do you charge for estimates?

No — on-site estimates are always free. Call (203) 895-3458 and Dennis will walk the property with you, talk through ideas, and follow up with a clear written quote.

Do you handle both design and construction?

Yes. BREN is a design-build company: the same person who designs your landscape — owner Dennis Leahy — builds it. Plans, plantings, stonework, lighting, and ongoing care, all from one point of contact.

When should I book a landscaping project in Fairfield County?

Spring and fall fill up fastest. For patios, firepits, and other masonry projects, reaching out a season ahead is ideal — but call anytime and we'll find a slot that works.
